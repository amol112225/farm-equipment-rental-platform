# Problem Statement

## Overview

Many farmers cannot afford expensive agricultural equipment such as tractors and harvesters. At the same time, many equipment owners have machinery that remains unused for a significant part of the year.

The current rental process relies on phone calls and personal contacts, making it difficult to find available equipment, compare prices, and manage bookings efficiently.

This project solves these problems by providing a centralized platform where farmers can rent equipment and equipment owners can list their machinery for others to use.

## Problems Identified

- No centralized rental platform
- Manual booking process
- Equipment availability is difficult to track
- No transparent pricing
- Scheduling conflicts

## Proposed Solution

Develop a web-based platform that allows:

- Equipment owners to list machinery
- Farmers to search and rent equipment
- Support for Instant Booking and Request Booking
- Easy booking and equipment managementnext
